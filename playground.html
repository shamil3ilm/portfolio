Fintech · Delegated access

Delegated account access

A service provider, like a payroll company, works on its customers' accounts. Access starts with a single-use key from the customer, every call is logged, and every refusal gives the same answer, while the log records the real reason.

Why it matters: a trusted provider can help run a customer's account, every action stays traceable, and access can be cut off instantly.

Try this: create a key, connect a provider with it, then create a payment and try to approve it.

          Tool · QR codes

          QR code maker

          Make a QR code for a website, Wi-Fi, a contact card and more. Add your logo if you like. Everything stays on your device.

          A QR code can't hold a picture itself, only a link to one. To share a photo, put it online and use a Link. Your logo is never uploaded.

          Tool · Encryption

          Lock & unlock text

          Lock a message with a password, or encode, fingerprint and scramble text in other common ways. Each type says honestly what it protects.

          Everything happens in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. The password lock uses AES-256 with a key stretched from your password, the same kind of protection used for saved passwords in real systems.
